### 02 — THE OPPORTUNITY
**Dubai loves tiramisù.**
**Nobody owns it.**
It is on almost every Italian menu in the city.
But there is no tiramisù brand.
Search "best tiramisù in Dubai" and you find lists, not a name.

> Visual: a grid of plain menu cards, each with "Tiramisù" as one line. One empty frame.

### 03 — WHAT PEOPLE TALK ABOUT
**Coffee. Balance. Texture.**
Strong coffee. Not too sweet. Creamy mascarpone.
Origin builds trust, but it is not what people talk about first.
*So we lead with the taste, and we let the origin prove it.*

> Visual: three giant words. "Origin" set small underneath, as the foundation.

### 04 — HOW A DESSERT BECOMES "THE ONE"
**The Dubai chocolate lesson.**
One signature product.
Made to be filmed.
One real creator moment.
Easy to get, anywhere in the city.
*Every mechanic in this strategy follows these four rules.*

> Visual: four steps on the timeline line. Small source note.

### 05 — WHERE WE ARE
**Downtown is not one audience.**
**Visitors:** 18.7 million came to Dubai in 2024. They ask, "What should we try here?"
**Residents:** they live and go out nearby. They ask, "Where do we go tonight?"
**Office workers:** from Business Bay and DIFC. They ask, "What do we bring to the meeting?"
**Opera guests:** around 250,000 in one season. They ask, "Where do we go after the show?"

> Visual: map of Downtown, four flows arriving at the venue, each with its question.

# CHAPTER 03 — THE FIRST BITE

### 12 — THE IDEA
**Your first bite wasn't your first bite.**
It is not only a launch line.
It is a campaign, a content series, a ritual in the venue and a number.

> Visual: the pitch's campaign reveal language (spoon, huge italic type).

### 13 — ALREADY LIVE: THE TEASER
**The accounts speak before the brand does.**
No logo. No product. Only a question.

> Visual: the three-post Instagram grid.
> **[POST 01: ASSET TBC]** "Do you remember your first bite?"
> **[POST 02: ASSET TBC]** "What if your first wasn't really your first?"
> **[POST 03: ASSET TBC]** *[Next line: to be defined]*
> A Story poll beside it: **"Do you remember your first tiramisù?"** Answers: **Yes** / ***Are you sure?***

### 14 — THE NUMBER
**Every first bite in Dubai is counted.**
Every first order comes with a numbered card: *FIRST BITE № 214.*
**№ 001** goes to the first person in line, not to a celebrity.
**№ 100 · № 1,000 · № 10,000**: every milestone is content, and a story for the press.
*The counter is also our scoreboard. It shows how many people came.*

> Visual: a row of numbered cards: № 001 · № 041 · № 214 · № 1,000. Reuse the pitch's № device.

### 15 — THE REACTION
**The pause. The expression. The second spoon.**
This is the format for every First Bite video: creators, guests, staff.
No script. No "it's so good."
We film the silence before the words.

> Visual: the pitch's three-frame film strip.

### 16 — THE CREATOR RULES
**Creators don't review. They take their first bite.**
Film the first bite, not the menu.
Say the name: Treviso Tiramisù, Dubai Opera district.
Keep your number card in the shot.
One personal code per creator, so we know who brings people in.
*Our direction for Nadia Werner's creators, so every voice tells one story.*

> Visual: the pitch's creator storyboard (the offer → the pause → the second spoon) with the rules written beside it, like notes on a script.

### 17 — THE SECOND BITE
**The first bite is a discovery.**
**The second bite is a decision.**
Bring your number back and your second bite comes with *[return offer: e.g. a coffee pairing or a new flavour, to be agreed]*.
This is how a first visit becomes a habit.

> Visual: two spoons on the table, one used and one clean. *(product/two-spoons-table)*

# CHAPTER 04 — PLATFORMS & CONTENT

### 18 — THE PLATFORMS
**Each platform has one job.**

| Platform | Its job | What we post | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | **The home.** Where people check us before they come. | The grid, Reels, Stories, highlights: menu, location, hours | Everyone. This is the brand's front door. |
| **TikTok** | **The discovery.** Where new people find us. | Street formats, reactions, First Bite Sessions, creators | Visitors, younger residents, people searching for "what to try" |
| **Meta ads** | **The push.** Our best content, shown to the right people near us. | Only content that already worked organically | People near Downtown, the Mall, Business Bay, DIFC and the Opera |
| **Google Maps** | **The decision.** Where "near me" ends. | Photos, hours, menu, answers to every review | People ready to walk in *(run by Treviso, with our direction)* |
| **Delivery apps** | **The second door.** Tiramisù at home, gifts to others. | Jars, boxes, the same photography | Residents and offices *(run by Treviso, with our direction)* |

> Visual: five tiles in one row, each with its job in one word: Home · Discovery · Push · Decision · Second door.

### 19 — OWN THE QUESTION
**People don't only scroll. They search.**
On TikTok, Instagram and Google, people ask: *"best tiramisù in Dubai."*
Every caption, video text and Maps profile uses the words people search: tiramisù, Dubai, original, Downtown, Dubai Opera.
We ask every guest and creator for a review, and we answer every review.
*This is how "tiramisù" starts to mean "Treviso."*

> Visual: a search field with "best tiramisu in dubai". The results show Treviso on TikTok, Instagram and Maps.

### 20 — THE CONTENT SERIES
**Four types of content. Each one is a series people recognise.**

| Content | Series | Job | Format | Posts / month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Product** | ***The Build:*** the Espresso Atelier in macro: pour, layer, dust. ***One spoon, every layer:*** cross-sections. | Make people hungry | Reels, carousels | 4 |
| **The First Bite** | ***First Bite Sessions:*** real reactions, guests and creators. ***The Number:*** milestones and cards. | Prove it | Reels, Stories | 4 |
| **In Dubai** | ***The Final Act:*** after the Opera. ***Are you sure?:*** street questions on the Boulevard. Gifting moments. | Make it relevant | Reels, Stories | 2 |
| **The Origin** | ***Treviso, 5:42 AM:*** short films from Treviso. ***Four ingredients:*** the recipe, the people. | Build trust | Carousels, short films | 2 |

*12 posts a month, 3 a week, same days and same quality. Reels are edited for Instagram and TikTok separately.*

### 22 — GIFTING, PART 1: THE FIRST BITE BOX
**For creators and press. Sent by Nadia Werner before opening.**
A numbered box: a jar of the classic, a card that says *"Your first bite wasn't your first bite,"* and an invitation.
**The box is the teaser. The invitation is the real gift.**
The unboxing goes online. The first bite happens at the venue, at the Espresso Atelier, on camera.

> Visual: a numbered box, open. Jar, card, invitation. *[PLACEHOLDER: box design]*

### 23 — GIFTING, PART 2: BRING THE ORIGINAL
**In the UAE, sweets are hospitality.**
**You don't arrive empty-handed.**
**The Treviso Box:** glass jars (6 or 12) or a whole tiramisù, ready to give.
**Occasions:** visiting family and friends. Celebrations. Office gifts from Business Bay and DIFC. Ramadan, Eid, National Day.
**Timing:** gift orders for Ramadan start 4 to 6 weeks early, so the box must launch before the season.
*The business plan already includes takeaway, breakfast boxes and corporate catering. Gifting gives them a story.*

> Visual: the box on a majlis table, next to coffee cups and dates. *[PLACEHOLDER: packaging]*

### 24 — PAID COMES AFTER
**We don't pay to find out what works. We pay to scale what already works.**
Paid starts on opening day, using only content that already worked in the four weeks before.
**Launch:** tell Dubai we are open.
**Discovery:** our best creator and First Bite videos.
**Footfall:** people near Downtown, Dubai Mall, Business Bay, DIFC and Dubai Opera.
**Reminder:** people who watched, engaged or visited our profile.
**Codes:** one code per campaign and one per creator (*FIRSTBITE* for dine-in), so every visit has a source.

> Visual: the pitch's two ad mocks ("Your first bite is waiting." / "The original, on us to start."), with the four stages drawn underneath.

### 25 — THE FIRST 90 DAYS
**First we tease. Then we tell. Then we seed. Then we pay.**

| Phase | When | Social (Inhaus) | Creators, PR & gifting (Nadia Werner) | Paid (Inhaus) | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · TEASE** | Now → week −3 | The 3-post grid. The "Are you sure?" poll. Street question on TikTok, with no brand | Creator list, press kit, First Bite Box production | Off | Curiosity. First followers. |
| **2 · TELL** | Weeks −3 → −2 | The reveal: *Treviso Tiramisù is coming to Dubai.* *Treviso, 5:42 AM.* The Build. The venue being built. Countdown. | Press announcement: the home of tiramisù arrives in Dubai | Off | People understand who we are. |
| **3 · SEED** | Weeks −2 → opening | Giveaway: 100 numbered opening invitations (№ 001–100). Free dine-in tiramisù. | First Bite Boxes delivered. Private creator preview at the venue; content held until opening day. | Off | A list of people waiting. Creator content ready. |
| **4 · OPEN** | Opening week | № 001. Real reactions every day. Street tastings with mini tiramisù on the Boulevard. | The activation: the shared first bite. All creator content goes live on the same day. Press at the venue. | **ON:** launch reach with the best teaser and creator content | A crowd, a queue, a moment. |
| **5 · AMPLIFY** | Weeks 2–4 | First Bite Sessions every week. Best guest videos reposted. Reviews. | Creators every week, each with a personal code | Discovery + footfall + codes. Scale the winners, stop the rest. | Steady visits. |
| **6 · RITUAL** | Weeks 5–8 | *The Final Act.* Coffee evenings. № 1,000. | Creators by occasion: date night, family, after the Opera | Reminder + footfall + delivery *[if live]* | Evenings become a habit. |
| **7 · GIFT & RETURN** | Weeks 9–12 | The Second Bite. The Treviso Box launch. | Gifting to community voices and offices. Gift guides in the press. | Gifting + past visitors | Repeat visits. First gift orders. |

*Day 90: we review the numbers together and plan the next season.*

> Visual: a wide timeline across the scroll (like the pitch's 156vw journey). Seven stops, and a line that switches from dotted to solid on opening day, when paid turns on.

### 26 — HOW WE MEASURE
**One question matters most:**
**When Dubai says tiramisù, does it say Treviso?**
**Searches:** "Treviso tiramisù" and "tiramisu Dubai" over time.
**The number:** how many first bites. Our live footfall count.
**Attention:** views, saves, shares. **Intent:** profile visits, directions, menu views.
**Visits:** codes used (per campaign and per creator), second bites, reviews, gift orders.

> Visual: one rising line, "Treviso × tiramisù", with the № counter beside it.
