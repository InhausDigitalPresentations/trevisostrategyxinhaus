### 02 — THE OPPORTUNITY
**Dubai has a famous tiramisù.**
**It doesn't have a tiramisù brand.**
Ask Google for the best tiramisù in Dubai. It answers with a restaurant dessert.
The best-known tiramisù in the city is one dish on a restaurant menu.
None of the names comes from Treviso.

> Visual: *This is what Dubai sees today.* A clean rebuild of the Google result for "best tiramisu in dubai" (Sept 2026): the AI answer names Public at Dubai Mall; the map list shows Public (4.6★, 7.7K reviews, Dubai Mall), Tiramisu Café (4.2★, 557, the Boulevard), The Guild (4.6★, 2.4K, DIFC). Treviso is not in it, yet.

### 03 — THE FIELD
**Who Dubai names today.**
**In our neighbourhood:** Public (Dubai Mall): the viral one, finished at the table. Tiramisu Café (the Boulevard): the café named after it. Eataly (Dubai Mall).
**Across the city:** La Fabbrica Italiana (Al Wasl) · Casa Mia (Garhoud) · Scalini (Jumeirah) · Il Borro (Al Naseem) · Vinci (JBR) · carine (Emirates Golf Club).
Three things we learn:
1. The most famous tiramisù in Dubai is a restaurant dish, not a brand.
2. "Original" is already used. La Fabbrica sells "The Original Tiramisu of 1958." We don't argue about who invented it. We own where it comes from.
3. The table show is common: Public, Casa Mia and Vinci all do it. A show alone is not a difference.

> Visual: nine small photos of the venues' tiramisù in a grid, split "Downtown" / "Across the city". Note: all checked open, Sept 2026. Torno Subito, still on older lists, closed in Nov 2024.

### 04 — WHAT PEOPLE TALK ABOUT
**Coffee. Balance. Texture.**
Strong coffee. Not too sweet. Creamy mascarpone.
Origin builds trust, but it is not what people talk about first.
*So we lead with the taste, and we let the origin prove it.*

> Visual: three giant words. "Origin" set small underneath, as the foundation.

### 05 — HOW A DESSERT BECOMES "THE ONE"
**The Dubai chocolate lesson.**
One signature product.
Made to be filmed.
One real creator moment.
Easy to get, anywhere in the city.
*Every mechanic in this strategy follows these four rules.*

> Visual: four steps on the timeline line. Small source note.

### 06 — WHERE WE ARE
**Downtown is not one audience.**
**Visitors:** 18.7 million came to Dubai in 2024. They ask, "What should we try here?"
**Residents:** they live and go out nearby. They ask, "Where do we go tonight?"
**Office workers:** from Business Bay and DIFC. They ask, "What do we bring to the meeting?"
**Opera guests:** around 250,000 in one season. They ask, "Where do we go after the show?"

> Visual: map of Downtown, four flows arriving at the venue, each with its question.

# CHAPTER 04 — PLATFORMS & CONTENT

### 19 — THE PLATFORMS
**Each platform has one job.**

| Platform | Its job | What we post | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | **The home.** Where people check us before they come. | The grid, Reels, Stories, highlights: menu, location, hours | Everyone. This is the brand's front door. |
| **TikTok** | **The discovery.** Where new people find us. | Street formats, reactions, First Bite Sessions, creators | Visitors, younger residents, people searching for "what to try" |
| **Meta ads** | **The push.** Our best content, shown to the right people near us. | Only content that already worked organically | People near Downtown, the Mall, Business Bay, DIFC and the Opera |
| **Google Maps** | **The decision.** Where "near me" ends. | Photos, hours, menu, answers to every review | People ready to walk in *(run by Treviso, with our direction)* |
| **Delivery apps** | **The second door.** Tiramisù at home, gifts to others. | Jars, boxes, the same photography | Residents and offices *(run by Treviso, with our direction)* |

> Visual: five tiles in one row, each with its job in one word: Home · Discovery · Push · Decision · Second door.

### 20 — OWN THE QUESTION
**Google already answers the question.**
**We change the answer.**
Google builds its answer from three sources. We work on all three:
**Creator videos:** Nadia Werner's creators say "best tiramisù in Dubai" and "Treviso Tiramisù" in the video and in the caption. The list includes the creators Google already quotes.
**Food lists and blogs:** PR sends the story to the people who write the lists (Emirates Woman, BBC Good Food ME, Curly Tales, Time Out Dubai, What's On, food bloggers).
**Google Maps:** photos, hours and menu from day one. We ask every guest for a review, and we answer every review.
*We won't pass Public's 7,700 reviews in 90 days. Being in the answer is realistic. Leading it is the Year 1 goal.*

> Visual: the search field "best tiramisu in dubai", with three columns under it: Creator videos · Lists & blogs · Google Maps.

### 23 — GIFTING, PART 1: THE FIRST BITE BOX
**For creators and press. Sent by Nadia Werner before opening.**
A numbered box: a jar of the classic, a card that says *"Your first bite wasn't your first bite,"* and an invitation.
**The box is the teaser. The invitation is the real gift.**
The unboxing goes online. The first bite happens at the venue, at the Espresso Atelier, on camera.

> Visual: a numbered box, open. Jar, card, invitation. *[PLACEHOLDER: box design]*

### 24 — GIFTING, PART 2: BRING THE ORIGINAL
**In the UAE, sweets are hospitality.**
**You don't arrive empty-handed.**
**The Treviso Box:** glass jars (6 or 12) or a whole tiramisù, ready to give.
**Occasions:** visiting family and friends. Celebrations. Office gifts from Business Bay and DIFC. Ramadan, Eid, National Day.
**Timing:** gift orders for Ramadan start 4 to 6 weeks early, so the box must launch before the season.
*The business plan already includes takeaway, breakfast boxes and corporate catering. Gifting gives them a story.*

> Visual: the box on a majlis table, next to coffee cups and dates. *[PLACEHOLDER: packaging]*

### 25 — PAID COMES AFTER
**We don't pay to find out what works. We pay to scale what already works.**
Paid starts on opening day, using only content that already worked in the four weeks before.
**Launch:** tell Dubai we are open.
**Discovery:** our best creator and First Bite videos.
**Footfall:** people near Downtown, Dubai Mall, Business Bay, DIFC and Dubai Opera.
**Reminder:** people who watched, engaged or visited our profile.
**Codes:** one code per campaign and one per creator (*FIRSTBITE* for dine-in), so every visit has a source.

> Visual: the pitch's two ad mocks ("Your first bite is waiting." / "The original, on us to start."), with the four stages drawn underneath.
